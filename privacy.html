Home Hub Backup: privacy policy

Home Hub Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses

It uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. This lets it create files in the owner's Google Drive and read, update or delete only those same files. It cannot see any other file.

What it stores

Once a day it uploads a backup of home-automation configuration. The backup is encrypted on the owner's device before upload. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. You can delete the backups by deleting the HomeHubBackup folder in Google Drive.
